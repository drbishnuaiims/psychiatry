Source: Matos Santana TE. Psychiatric Aspects of Perioperative Pain. In: Zimbrean PC et al., editors. Perioperative Psychiatry. Springer Nature Switzerland AG; 2019. Chapter 6, pp. 65–83. This note is a structured synthesis of the uploaded chapter; it is not a substitute for current anaesthesia, acute-pain, addiction-medicine or institutional protocols.

Important source limitation: The chapter reflects evidence and recommendations available around 2019. In particular, do not apply its historical suggestion to stop buprenorphine 24 hours before surgery as a current default. Contemporary perioperative buprenorphine decisions should be made with anaesthesia/acute-pain and addiction teams using current local guidance. Drug doses and opioid-equivalence conversions are intentionally not reproduced as stand-alone prescribing instructions.
